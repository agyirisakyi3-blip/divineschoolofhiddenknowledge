How to join us

One form, a phone call, and a visit if you want one. Everything below is what actually happens, including the parts that are less convenient.

Process

Six steps

Most applicants complete this in three to four weeks. The slow part is usually document verification, not us.

  1. Submit the form

    Online, below. You get an acknowledgement reference immediately. Nothing is payable at this stage.

  2. We call you

    A counsellor calls within two working days to understand your background, and to answer questions honestly — including whether a particular programme is right for you.

  3. Upload documents

    Scanned certificates, proof of income if you are applying for a bursary, and a recent photograph. We tell you exactly which ones we still need.

  4. Campus visit (optional)

    If you would like to see the theatre, the pharmacy and the gardens before deciding, we will arrange it. We think you should.

  5. Merit list

    Applications are ranked on academic merit, with reserved-category and scholarship criteria applied as per regulation.

  6. Offer and first instalment

    You receive an offer letter. Confirm your seat by paying the first instalment before the date stated in it.

Eligibility

Who can apply

These are our own entry requirements — there is no affiliating university to defer to. Confirm them with the admissions office before you apply, as we may adjust entry standards as the programmes develop.

Minimum academic qualification by programme.
Programme Minimum qualification Subject requirement Min. age
Professional Diploma in Ayurvedic Medicine Senior secondary (WASSCE / O-Level / A-Level) or equivalent Biology, or Science 17 years
Advanced Diploma in Ayurvedic Practice Any diploma in Ayurveda or a related therapy field — 21 years
Diploma in Panchakarma Therapy Diploma in a therapy field, or professional practice — —
Diploma in Ayurvedic Herbal Pharmacy Senior secondary or equivalent Science 17 years
Diploma in Ayurvedic Dietetics & Nutrition Senior secondary or equivalent Biology 17 years
Diploma in Yoga Therapy Open to all, including practising therapists — —
Certificate in Ayurveda & Wellness None Open to all 16 years

These requirements are admission criteria for our own training programmes only. They are not a route to professional registration — see what our awards are.

Documents required

  • BECE / O-Level certificate, or equivalent
  • WASSCE / A-Level certificate, or equivalent
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • National ID or birth certificate
  • Proof of income, if applying for a bursary
  • Certificate from a prior diploma or therapy course, for advanced applicants
  • Passport bio-data page and student visa, for international applicants

Scanned copies are fine at application stage. Originals are verified at the time of admission.

Scholarships & bursaries

  • Means-cum-merit scholarship
  • Need-based bursary, applied for separately
  • Staff and alumni-sponsored awards
  • Instalment plans on request, no interest

Scholarship decisions are made by committee after admission and are applied retrospectively to the year of admission.

Hostel accommodation

  • Separate boys' and girls' hostels, both on campus
  • Double and shared rooms; limited single rooms
  • Mess with sattvic and Ayurveda-appropriate menus
  • Warden in residence, CCTV, and visiting hours
  • Charged per academic year, mess billed separately — figures on request

Rooms are allotted after admission is confirmed, on the basis of application date and availability. Current rates are sent with your offer letter.
