The School

A Sanctuary for Revealing
Sacred Truths

The Divine School of Hidden Knowledge is a structured institution designed to lift the veil off spiritual and physical mysteries.

D.S.O.H.K represents a formal sanctuary dedicated to revealing sacred, esoteric truths — truths hidden from casual observation but essential for spiritual mastery and physical well-being. Here, the unseen is not merely studied but lived: through disciplined prayer, disciplined study, and disciplined application of the laws that govern both the inner landscape of the human spirit and the outer landscape of physical health.

Guided by divine order, we equip seekers with the wisdom to master their inner spiritual nature and their outer physical life as two expressions of a single creation. Students learn to integrate timeless truths with purposeful living — so that knowledge becomes illumination, and illumination becomes character.

“My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, and thou shalt forget the law of thy God.”

Hosea 4:6 — KJV

At D.S.O.H.K, we reclaim that knowledge — not as information to be amassed, but as revelation that transforms mind, heart, and action.

The Pillars of Knowledge

Six Pillars of the
Discipline

A curriculum that bridges the seen and the unseen.

✦

Spiritual Mastery

Cultivate inner awareness, discernment, and alignment with divine principles. Awaken the latent potential within through prayer, meditation, and sacred study.

✦

Divine Mysteries

Uncover the hidden laws that govern creation. Explore sacred symbols, esoteric wisdom, and the deeper meanings found in scripture and ancient teaching.

✦

Herbal Medicine

Study the medicinal intelligence of plants — their constitution, energetics, and rightful use — as a divine provision for the healing of the body.

✦

Inner Alchemy

Transform character, refine the heart, and purify intention. Experience growth through disciplined practice and the transmutation of the self.

✦

Sacred Order

Learn to live in harmony with divine order — restoring balance between spirit and matter, faith and wisdom, contemplation and action.

✦

Illumination

Receive and embody knowledge that enlightens. Move beyond intellectual understanding into living revelation.
