Programmes

Seven programmes, one standard

Professional diplomas and certificates in clinical therapy, herbal pharmacy, dietetics and yoga — taught by practising vaidyas for people who want the clinical work, not just a title.

What these qualifications are. Every programme on this page awards a diploma or certificate of training issued by this college. We are a private training college and we are not accredited, so none of these awards is a university degree and none of them on its own entitles a holder to register or practise as a healthcare practitioner in Ghana or any other country.

They are designed for therapists, and for people who want the clinical grounding rather than a title. Ask us before you enrol if you need a formally recognised qualification — we will tell you straight whether we can help.

Curriculum

How the Professional Diploma is structured

The first two years build the base — Sanskrit, philosophy and the biomedical sciences you will be examined in. The middle years turn that into practice. The final year is a full internship.

Apply for the diploma

  1. Years 1–2 · The foundation

    Classical Sanskrit, Ayurvedic philosophy, Dravyaguna, Sharira sthana and modern anatomy, physiology and biochemistry. This is where non-Sanskrit speakers find their footing.

  2. Years 3–4 · The science of treatment

    Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita in the original, Rasashastra and Bhaishajya Kalpana, Kayachikitsa, with supervised ward work from year three.

  3. Years 4–5 · Specialties and practice

    Panchakarma, Shalakritantra, Agadatantra, Stri Roga, Balrog and the emerging disciplines of yoga therapy and integrative medicine.

  4. Year 6 · Internship

    Twelve months of full-time clinical posting in the attached hospital, with a logbook, assessed competencies and a final examination.
