The discipline

Four ideas the whole curriculum is built on

Ayurveda is not a list of remedies. It is a structured way of understanding a person — their constitution, their environment, and what keeps them well. These four ideas appear in every year of every programme we run.

01

Tridosha

Every person carries Vata, Pitta and Kapha in their own proportion. Diagnosis begins with finding yours, and treatment begins with keeping it in balance.

02

Panchakarma

The five principal purification procedures — controlled vomiting, purging, enemas, nasal and oil therapy — taught to clinical standard in our own theatre.

03

Dashamoola

The ten classical roots that anchor much of Ayurvedic pharmacy. Students process, identify and dose them rather than memorising a list.

04

Purushottama

“That which is best for the person.” Treatment is chosen for the individual in front of you, never for the disease label alone.
